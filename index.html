Common questions

My other device isn't up to date. Check you're signed into the same iCloud account on both, and that Cairn's iCloud switch is on in Settings → iCloud. Opening Cairn fetches the latest.

I've lost my backup password. It can't be recovered: Cairn never sees it. Make a new backup with a new password.

Where do I put my password? In your password manager. Cairn stores the website and username so it can open the right sign-in page; your password manager fills in the rest.

Can my partner see my items? Only the households you share with them.
