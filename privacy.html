Privacy policy

Cairn: Home & Life Management · Last updated 9 November 2026

Cairn is made by an independent developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version: Cairn doesn't collect any data, and the developer can't see anything you put in it.

Where your information is kept

  • On your device, in Cairn's own storage, protected by your device's encryption.
  • In your own iCloud account, if you use iCloud sync. Everything you enter (items, dates, prices, notes, account details and fields of your own) travels in iCloud's end-to-end encrypted fields, and documents are encrypted on your device before they're uploaded. iCloud sees only which kind of record it is, which household it belongs to and when it changed. The developer has no access to any of it.
  • If you share a household, its information is also visible to the people you share it with, through their iCloud. You control this and can stop sharing at any time.
  • In backup files you create, encrypted with a password only you know, saved wherever you choose.
  • In your calendar, only if you turn on Settings → Calendar → Act-by dates in Calendar: act-by dates go into the calendar you choose, and that calendar's own service keeps them. With a lock on they say only "Cairn: a decision is due".

Reading letters and statements

When you photograph a letter or open a bank statement, Cairn reads it on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. A statement isn't kept once you've chosen what to add.

What Cairn never does

  • It never stores passwords, card numbers or bank details.
  • No Cairn accounts, no analytics, no adverts, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • It never sends your information to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Cairn Plus is bought through Apple. Cairn never sees your payment details.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Apple's iCloud sends silent signals so your devices know to sync; these carry no personal content.

Deleting your data

In Cairn: Settings → Erase everything removes your Cairn data from this device, your other devices and iCloud. Households someone else shared with you stay theirs. You can also delete Cairn's iCloud data in iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Cairn.

Children

Cairn isn't aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
