Support

Help with Cairn, straight from the developer.

Email cairnhelp@outlook.com. Please say which device you're using and what you expected to happen. Never send passwords, card numbers or bank details.

Common questions

What’s an act-by date? The last day you can still switch, haggle or cancel: the end date less the notice period, the time a switch takes, or the window to shop around, whichever is longest. Reminders count down to it.

How do I share with my family? In the ••• menu: Family → a household → Invite someone. Each person needs an Apple Account and Cairn on their own device. Everyone in the household sees the same items and documents.

My devices don’t match. Check each one is signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud allowed for Cairn (Settings → your name → iCloud → Saved to iCloud → See All → Cairn), then open Cairn on each. Settings → iCloud shows when it last synced.

What does Cairn Plus add? Free covers up to 15 items you own, with every feature. Cairn Plus (£6.99 a month or £49.99 a year, each with a 14-day free trial) removes the limit and works with Family Sharing.

What happens if Cairn Plus ends? Nothing is deleted. 15 of your items stay fully active, your choice or the soonest dates. The rest pause: you can still see, export, back up and delete them, but they don’t alert and can’t be edited until Plus comes back. Households someone else shared with you are never affected.

How do I cancel Cairn Plus? In iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or in Cairn: Settings → Cairn Plus → Manage subscription.

Can I add my own fields? Yes. In an item’s form, tap + Add detail → Your own… and choose text, number, money, date, phone, link or email. A date can remind you. Swipe a field to remove it.

Can Cairn track memberships? Yes. Add → Subscription or membership: gyms, clubs, the National Trust, streaming. Costs has a check-up that shows what you pay each month and what overlaps.

School fees are paid once a term. Can Cairn do that? Yes. In the price, choose how often: monthly, yearly, once a term (with each term’s date), certain months of the year (council tax over ten months, say) or set dates. Costs and the year ahead count it the way it’s really paid.

Can one item cover several people? Yes. In the form, Covers → tick everyone it’s for, or choose Everyone in the household. In Costs → By person, shared items are split evenly between them, or switch on “Show the full amount under each person”.

Can I hide the amounts? Yes, for showing the app to someone. Tap the eye in the top bar (iPad and Mac), or ••• → Hide amounts (iPhone), or ⌘⇧H on a Mac. Every figure reads £•••, on your widgets too, until you tap again. It’s per device (per person, on a device several people share), and isn’t a lock: use Settings → Lock for that.

Can I try Cairn before adding my own? Yes. On first launch, or in ••• → About & help (on iPad and Mac, About & help in the sidebar), choose “Explore with a sample family”. Nothing you do there touches your own items, and “Leave the sample” brings you back.

Can several of us use one iPad or Mac? Yes (an iPhone too). First make sure Cairn knows who you are (••• → This is me), then ••• → Settings → This iPad (or This Mac) → Shared by several people, and tick who uses it. “Who’s using Cairn?” then comes up when Cairn opens and after time away (15 minutes unless you choose otherwise). Each person gets their own settings (Hide amounts, Light or Dark, filters, how an item’s page is arranged), and Cairn notes who changed what (“Edited by Emma on the Family iPad”). Switch from your avatar (in the sidebar on iPad, the top bar on a Mac), ••• → Switch person…, or ⌘⇧P on a Mac. While someone else is using it, the owner’s Only-me items are hidden everywhere, widgets and Spotlight included. Only the device’s owner can turn sharing off.

Who does Cairn Plus belong to on a shared device? The device stays signed in to one Apple Account, and Cairn’s data, iCloud, Only-me items and Cairn Plus belong to that account. So on a shared device it’s Plus for everyone using it, and everyone’s items count towards the same 15 free ones.

What does a PIN do? Each person can lock their profile with a 4-digit PIN: ••• → Your profile → PIN → Lock my profile with a PIN. Cairn then asks for it when someone picks them on a shared device. Five wrong tries mean a minute’s wait, and the wait doubles after that. The PIN is kept only in a scrambled form (a slow, salted hash), never as the digits. It’s a curtain between family members, not encryption: anyone who knows the device’s own passcode can remove it.

I’ve forgotten my PIN. On the PIN pad, tap Forgot it?. Cairn asks for the device’s own Face ID, Touch ID or passcode (on a Mac, Touch ID or its login password). Once that’s given, your PIN is removed and your profile opens; set a new one in ••• → Your profile → PIN. The PIN travels with you, so removing it removes it on every device. The device’s owner can also remove anyone’s PIN: Family → that person → Remove their PIN, confirmed with the device’s passcode. There’s no other way round it, and the developer can’t reset it for you, because nothing is kept on a server.

Can I sort items into areas, like the car or the house? Yes. Each household has areas (Home, Cars, Phones & tech…), sub-areas such as Kitchen, and things such as “Tesla Model Y” or “Boiler”, each thing with its own page, documents and yearly cost. Cairn gives each item a starting area from its kind; it never puts an item in a thing without asking. Change it in the item’s form, with Move to…, by choosing several in All, or by dragging onto the sidebar on iPad and Mac. Edit areas is in the sidebar, and in Family → a household on iPhone.

What happens if I delete an area or a thing? No item is ever deleted with it. Deleting an area moves its items to “No area”. Deleting a thing leaves its items in the thing’s area and asks about the thing’s own documents: keeping them is the default, and they then show in the household’s Documents as “from Tesla Model Y (deleted)”.

Can I change the order of an item’s page? Yes. On an item, tap ≡ on Details, Your fields or Account to drag its rows, or Arrange sections (at the top on iPad and Mac, in ••• on iPhone) to drag whole sections. Pin up to three details (a registration, a policy number) to show beside the act-by date, and optionally in your lists. It’s per kind of item and per person, and follows you to your other devices. Reset puts Cairn’s order back.

What does Cairn read from a letter or agreement? On a new item, tap Fill in from a document, then Scan, Choose a file or Choose a photo. Cairn reads the words on your device, using a PDF’s own text or Apple’s text recognition for a photo or scan, and, where your device has it, Apple’s on-device model. Nothing is sent anywhere. It looks for the kind of item, the provider, the price and how often it’s paid, the dates, a reference, a phone number and a website; for car finance (PCP, HP or a loan), the monthly repayments, the final payment, the APR, the mileage allowance and the car. If it can’t be sure (car finance or car insurance, say), it asks you. Everything it found is marked, and you check it before anything is saved.

What does Cairn never read? Card numbers, bank account numbers, sort codes and IBANs are replaced with dots before anything reads the page, Apple’s on-device model included. Dates of birth, occupations, employment details and addresses are blanked the same way. A certificate’s or licence’s own number isn’t taken. When you save, Cairn keeps the details you checked and the document itself, filed with its item and encrypted like your other documents, much as you’d keep the paper. It doesn’t keep the words it read separately. A bank statement you import isn’t kept at all.

How do I back up? Settings → Backup → Back up now makes one file with everything, locked with a password you choose. Keep the password somewhere safe: without it the file can’t be opened, by you or by the developer.

How do I delete everything? Settings → Erase everything. It can’t be undone.
