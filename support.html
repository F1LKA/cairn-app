Support

Help with Cairn, straight from the developer.

Email cairnhelp@outlook.com. Please say which device you're using and what you expected to happen. Never send passwords, card numbers or bank details.

Common questions

What's an act-by date? The last day you can still switch, haggle or cancel: the end date less the notice period, the time a switch takes, or the window to shop around, whichever is longest. Reminders count down to it.

How do I share with my family? In the ••• menu: Family → a household → Invite someone. Each person needs an Apple Account and Cairn on their own device. Everyone in the household sees the same items and documents.

My devices don't match. Check each one is signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud allowed for Cairn (Settings → your name → iCloud → Apps using iCloud), then open Cairn on each. Settings → iCloud shows when it last synced.

What does Cairn Plus add? Free covers up to 15 items you own, with every feature. Cairn Plus (£6.99 a month or £49.99 a year, each with a 14-day free trial) removes the limit and works with Family Sharing.

What happens if Cairn Plus ends? Nothing is deleted. 15 of your items stay fully active, your choice or the soonest dates. The rest pause: you can still see, export, back up and delete them, but they don't alert and can't be edited until Plus comes back. Households someone else shared with you are never affected.

How do I cancel Cairn Plus? In iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or in Cairn: Settings → Cairn Plus → Manage subscription.

Can I add my own fields? Yes. In an item's form, tap + Add detail → Your own… and choose text, number, money, date, phone or link. A date can remind you. Swipe a field to remove it.

How do I back up? Settings → Backup → Back up now makes one file with everything, locked with a password you choose. Keep the password somewhere safe: without it the file can't be opened, by you or by the developer.

How do I delete everything? Settings → Erase everything. It can't be undone.
