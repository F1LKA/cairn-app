How Cairn keeps your information safe

There's no Cairn server, so there's nothing of yours for anyone to break into but your own devices and your own iCloud.

On your devices

  • Cairn's data sits in its own storage, encrypted by your device whenever it's locked.
  • Documents you add are kept with the strongest protection your device offers: they can't be read until you unlock it.
  • An optional lock (Settings → Lock) asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode before showing documents and account details, or before opening Cairn at all. With it on, alerts, widgets, the calendar, Spotlight and Siri name nothing.

In iCloud

  • Everything you enter travels in iCloud's end-to-end encrypted fields, and documents are encrypted on your device (AES-256) before they leave it.
  • Each household has its own keys, shared only with the people you share that household with.
  • Apple can't read it, and neither can the developer.

What Cairn never holds

  • No passwords: Open account hands sign-in to your password manager.
  • No card numbers or bank details. A policy or customer reference is the most it keeps, and it shows as •••• until you tap it.

Backups

A backup is one file you keep, sealed with your password (AES-256, with the password stretched 600,000 times so guessing is slow). Cairn never keeps the password. Lose it and the file can't be opened.

What it relies on

Cairn uses Apple's own encryption and iCloud, and no other company's code. Its safety depends on your device's passcode and your Apple Account, so use a strong passcode and two-factor authentication.

Reporting a problem

If you think you've found a security problem, email cairnhelp@outlook.com.
